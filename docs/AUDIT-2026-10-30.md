# Taksh website audit — 30 October 2026

Scope: the public home page, catalogue, six product pages, Quality, Help, contact and missing-piece forms, returns/privacy pages, shopping flow, shared navigation/footer/cookie controls, redirects and 404. Owner customer records were not audited. Objective fixes are implemented; subjective recommendations below are proposals, not approved copy/design changes.

## Method and grading

Review dimensions: clarity (what this is), hierarchy (what to look at first), relevance (does it help choose/build), trust (evidence and limitations), action/flow (next step and recovery), and mobile/accessibility. Performance is reported separately from editorial grades. Panel grades are reviewer judgments, not conversion measurements: A = strong/keep; B = useful with a specific weakness; C = substantial friction or unclear value; D = trust/commercial-readiness blocker. No sales or abandonment uplift is claimed.

Reviewed live content and local production build. Browser layout checks cover 14 public pages at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px (56 combinations). All had one main-document H1, no horizontal document overflow and no missing alt attributes on rendered main images. This is not full accessibility certification; empty decorative alt values are intentional, and text contrast, native mobile keyboards and assistive-technology usability were not exhaustively measured.

## Implemented objective fixes

1. **Gallery enlargement.** Reproduced Yellow Taxi's 900 px selected image remaining fixed in a 1,384 px viewer. The selection hook now releases the old responsive candidate when entering/leaving a viewer or resizing. Retest selected the largest available 1,254 px source. No invented pixels or original-image edits. Shared correction also covers Ram Mandir, Bharat, Chandrayaan and Nonsense. Gully's independent original-image viewer remains intact.
2. **Startup taps.** Gully build thumbnails and gallery tools now join the existing one-tap startup buffer. Latest choice is replayed once when ready; cart actions/forms remain excluded.
3. **Scroll work.** Removed the sticky-purchase hook's per-scroll listener and its two explicit geometry reads. Intersection/resize observers determine visibility. A fast-scroll test exposed a boundary case in the first candidate; extending the observer's lower boundary corrected it. Final mobile jump from top to 2,000 px shows the bar, and returning to top hides it.
4. **Network recovery.** Cart syncing has an 8-second request timeout; checkout requests have 20 seconds, covering response body as well as headers. Errors return control to the visitor. No automatic submission retries. This bounds individual stalled requests, not the total checkout initialization time across multiple sequential calls.
5. **Support upload validation.** Reject unsupported formats and photos above 5 MB immediately, before upload. Keep server byte-signature validation. Missing set moves focus to its selector; support success is announced as a status.
6. **Navigation semantics.** Current primary-navigation page/section is now marked for assistive technology on desktop and mobile.
7. **Gully public cache coverage.** Gully was missing from the public-page cache allowlist. It now receives the same anonymous-document caching as the other products. Build-specific cache keys remain; cart/checkout/owner/unknown cookies, authenticated requests and partial React responses still bypass it.

Prices, ages, piece counts, stock policy, approved product copy and image originals were not changed. Analytics remains on by default per the user's earlier explicit direction.

## Highest-priority qualitative findings

- **Purchase intent and fulfilment do not match.** Product pages/cards say Add to Cart, bag says Checkout, but the next page reveals all items are out of stock and collects a request. State the actual transaction near the initial decision, and use a consistent action name through the journey. This requires a deliberate business/copy decision.
- **Privacy page remains visibly unfinished.** Legal name, address, email, date, grievance contact and retention/rights sections have placeholders. Earlier instructions explicitly deferred these facts. They remain deferred; do not invent them. This audit is not a legal opinion.
- **Quality promises outrun the available proof.** The page promises numbered bags, a printed book and spares in every box. Earlier product verification was deferred. Verify the promise for every SKU and support it with an actual box/instruction photograph before relying on it as conversion reassurance.
- **A stock-update request asks for too much while requiring too little.** Full address occupies a large form, yet contact details may all be omitted. Propose contact-first, optional delivery details later, and a deliberate policy on whether at least one contact method is required.
- **Product truth needs one consistent standard.** Gully has a preview notice and no-lights disclaimer; other product pages use different treatments. Imagined scenes are valuable storytelling, but should not carry the burden of proving the final physical set.

## Validation and limits

- Automated tests: 59 pass after the cache regression was added.
- TypeScript and production build pass; performance budgets pass.
- Global CSS: 105,146 bytes / 20,055 gzip bytes. Existing thumbnail rows: about 22–47 KB; each under the 80 KB budget. No claim that this pass reduced the CSS bundle.
- All 17 Gully original files retain exact bytes; optimized display variants remain in use.
- 56 responsive document checks: no overflow, one main H1, no omitted image alt attributes.
- 21 local route checks: 16 normal routes returned 200, four aliases returned 308 to home, unknown route returned 404. Bag/checkout headings are client-rendered and were inspected separately.
- All six Gully build choices and all nine original viewer images checked. Previous/next, Escape and keyboard controls were exercised; no new physical touchscreen/swipe test was performed.
- All six home feature selections target the correct product. Gully uses the five-build overview in the feature; product cards retain the single Terrace House image.
- Filter combination and browser-back restoration passed. Analytics opt-out persisted after reload in the local browser.
- Local cart integration: server-authoritative prices, quantity, draft-to-request lifecycle and protection from later cart overwrite passed. Gully quantity two totals Rs 5,998. No production request or contact message was submitted.
- Desktop viewer resolution was verified before/after; mobile sticky bar tested with a 2,000 px scroll jump and return to top.
- Slow-network tooling limitation: the pre-navigation performance hook was unsupported. Buffered entries were available, but requested throttling produced a 16 ms local TTFB despite a 150 ms latency setting. Therefore that sample is not accepted as a validated throttled benchmark, and no slow-network LCP or speed-uplift claim is made. Network timeout behaviour is tested deterministically instead.
- Historical, geographic, product-packaging and legal claims were assessed for clarity/evidence gaps, not independently fact-checked. Real phones, screen readers, production customer records and Google Sheet delivery were not tested in this pass.
